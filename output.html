# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training Recovery

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to support marathon training recovery with high energy availability for endurance performance:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (140g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (20g), and whole milk (240g). Side of whole wheat toast (50g) with almond butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Greek yogurt (200g) with granola (40g) and mixed berries (100g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 45g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g) with brown rice (150g cooked) and roasted sweet potato (200g). Mixed green salad (150g) with olive oil dressing (15g).
    * Protein: 45g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 85g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Rice cakes (50g) with honey (20g) and banana (120g).
    * Protein: 3g, Fat: 1g, Carbs: 65g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Baked salmon fillet (160g) with whole wheat pasta (120g cooked) and marinara sauce (100g). Steamed broccoli (200g) with olive oil (10g).
    * Protein: 40g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 85g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Cottage cheese (150g) with pineapple chunks (100g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 4g, Carbs: 20g

This meal plan prioritizes high-quality carbohydrates for sustained energy, adequate protein for muscle recovery, and nutrient-dense foods to support the demands of marathon training and recovery.
